# --- Chrom's jab combo -------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Pikachu has attack_11 only. Chrom's jab chains 1 -> 2 -> 3 with a rapid-jab branch, and his
# attack_11 deliberately does NOT set FIGHTER_STATUS_ATTACK_FLAG_ENABLE_RESTART (Pikachu's does,
# on frame 7, which is what makes his jab repeatable). Porting Chrom's jab faithfully therefore
# leaves a jab that can neither repeat nor chain -- a real flow regression with no interim fix
# that preserves parity. These animations plus synthesised motions plus
# StatusModule::change_status_request_from_script (workshop episode 10) close it.
